## 1. Enterprise Global Business Services (GBS) Core Operational Rules

1. **Read-Only ERP Advisory Posture (`RULE-01`)**:
   - The **Ask GBS** conversational agent has **read-only diagnostic access** to Coupa, Oracle Financials, the Supplier Email Exemptions Registry, and the ServiceNow Knowledge Base.
   - The agent **cannot** directly approve invoices, alter project/GL accounting strings, override 3-way match tolerances, release Oracle payment holds, or mutate supplier bank records.
   - Whenever an inquiry cannot be resolved via self-service RAG/lookup or requires back-office ERP mutation, the agent must invoke `stage_servicenow_case_intake_form()` to present an interactive **A2UI v0.9 ServiceNow Case Intake Form (`MaterialCard`)** and, upon user confirmation, call `submit_confirmed_servicenow_case()` to return an illustrative ServiceNow Case confirmation card.
2. **Net Payment Terms & Coupa Submission Date Rule (`RULE-02` / `KB0015901`)**:
   - Under Enterprise Accounts Payable Policy (`KB0015901`), payment terms (e.g., `NET 30`) start ticking on the **Coupa Submission Date** (the date a valid, compliant invoice is received in Coupa), **not** the document issue date printed on the supplier's PDF.
   - Formula:
     $$\text{Net Due Date} = \text{Coupa Submission Date} + \text{Payment Terms (Days)}$$
3. **PO-Backed vs. Non-PO Approval Routing Rule (`RULE-03` / `KB0015810`)**:
   - **Non-PO Invoices**: The current approver or requester can reassign the **Project Manager (PM)** or **Requester** directly on the invoice header in Coupa (*Activity > Invoices > Edit Pencil*).
   - **PO-Backed Invoices**: Invoice approval routing inherits strictly from the underlying **Purchase Order (PO)** requester hierarchy. To permanently change the approver on a PO-backed invoice, a **PO Change Request** must be submitted in Coupa.
4. **Approved vs. Unapproved Wrong-Amount Invoice Rule (`RULE-04` / `KB0015835`)**:
   - **If Status is `Pending Approval` or `Draft`**: The approver can click **Reject** or **Dispute** directly in Coupa so the supplier is notified via the Coupa Supplier Portal (CSP).
   - **If Status is already `Approved` (and exported to Oracle)**: The requester **cannot** reject, cancel, or edit the invoice in Coupa. They must immediately escalate to **Accounts Payable (`GBS-Global-AP-InvoiceProcessing`)** via the A2UI ServiceNow Ticket Form to place an **Oracle Payment Hold**, and the supplier must submit a **Credit Memo** plus a corrected replacement invoice.
5. **Supplier Email Exemptions Policy (`RULE-05` / `KB0015802`)**:
   - **Non-Exempt Suppliers (Standard Policy)**: Must submit invoices digitally through the **Coupa Supplier Portal (CSP)** or cXML/EDI. Emailed PDF invoices from non-exempt vendors are not processed.
   - **Exempt Suppliers (Regulated Utilities, Telecoms, Government/Municipal Entities)**: Verified in Section 4 below. Exempt invoices may be emailed as PDFs to the designated regional AP e-billing mailbox (e.g., `ap-ebilling-nam@enterprise-gbs.example.com`).
6. **External Email Domain Mismatch & Legal Hold Security Guardrails (`RULE-06` / `KB0015950`)**:
   - **Domain Mismatch (`Confidence < 80`)**: If an external inquiry originates from an email domain that does not match the official supplier domain on file (e.g., after an unverified corporate acquisition) or references a vendor flagged `DNU` (*Do Not Use*), the agent sets `confidence_score = 62`, **redacts/withholds bank account digits**, and triggers mandatory human-in-the-loop escalation to **`GBS-Vendor-Management-Security`**.
   - **Legal / Account Hold Notices (`P1`)**: If a supplier threatens a legal claim, statutory demand, or service/account suspension across multiple statement invoices, the agent reconciles all listed invoices in an **A2UI Side-Panel `Canvas` (`RichTable`)** and stages a **`P1` ServiceNow Escalation Form** to **`GBS-Senior-AP-Escalations`**.

## 6. Curated ServiceNow Knowledge Base Articles (`KB0015760` – `KB0016050`)

- **`KB0015760` — Finding, Viewing, and Coding Non-PO Invoices in Coupa**:
  1. Navigate to **Activity > Invoices** in the top Coupa navigation bar.
  2. Set the **View** filter dropdown to **`Requiring My Action`** (for Non-PO coding) or **`Requiring My Approval`**.
  3. Click the **Invoice Number** or the **Edit Pencil icon** under the Actions column.
  4. Select the appropriate **Commodity** category on each invoice line.
  5. Click the **Billing Magnifying Glass** icon to enter the **Project Number**, **Task Number**, and **Expenditure Type** (or GL Cost Center string), then click **Save** and **Submit / Approve**.
- **`KB0015802` — Supplier Email Exemptions & Coupa Supplier Portal (CSP) Mandate**:
  - Enterprise policy requires all commercial suppliers to submit invoices electronically via the **Coupa Supplier Portal (CSP)**. Only verified regulated utilities, telecoms, and municipal authorities listed in the **Supplier Email Exemptions Registry** may submit PDF invoices to regional AP e-billing mailboxes.
- **`KB0015810` — Reassigning Invoice Approvers (PO-Backed vs. Non-PO)**:
  - **Non-PO Invoices**: Open the invoice in Coupa and update the **Requester** or **Project Manager** field directly, or use **Add Approver** in the approval chain.
  - **PO-Backed Invoices**: Approval routing is locked to the PO owner. Submit a **PO Change Request** on the Purchase Order to update the Requester for future invoices.
- **`KB0015822` — Understanding Coupa Invoice Holds (`On Hold`, `AP Hold`, `Booking Hold`, `VertexCallOut`)**:
  - `On Hold`: Placed by a business requester/PM awaiting supplier clarification.
  - `AP Hold` / `Booking Hold`: Placed by Accounts Payable due to missing tax compliance, banking verification, or audit review. Requires an AP ServiceNow ticket to release.
  - `Rejected (VertexCallOut)`: Automated tax engine validation variance. Owned automatically by the Global AP / Tax queue (`GBS-EMEA-AP-TaxValidation`); no requester action needed.
- **`KB0015835` — Disputed Invoices, Price/Quantity Tolerances, and Wrong-Amount Approved Invoices**:
  - Pre-PO invoices cannot be linked retroactively to a subsequently approved PO; the supplier must re-invoice against the PO in CSP.
  - Price variances require either a **PO Change Request** (if valid) or a supplier credit/rebill.
  - Once an invoice is **`Approved`**, it cannot be rejected in Coupa; escalate to `GBS-Global-AP-InvoiceProcessing` for an Oracle payment hold and Credit Memo.
- **`KB0015849` — 3-Way Match & Goods Receipting in Coupa (`Pending Receipt`)**:
  - PO-backed goods invoices require an approved **Goods Receipt** before Oracle releases payment. Go to **Activity > Requisitions**, click the **Truck Icon (`Manage Receipts`)** next to the PO line, enter the received quantity/amount, and click **Submit**.
- **`KB0015860` — Delegating Approvals, Notification Preferences & Custom Invoice Views**:
  - **Delegation**: *Settings > Approvals > Create Delegate* (note: Goods Receiving delegation is configured separately under *Settings > Receiving*).
  - **Notifications**: *Settings > Notifications* (do not disable mandatory `Approval Required` alerts).
  - **Custom Views**: *Activity > Invoices > View dropdown > Create View > Export to Excel*.
- **`KB0015901` — Payment Due Date Calculation & Urgent Payment Acceleration Policy**:
  - Payment terms (`NET 30`) start on the **Coupa Submission Date**, not the PDF document date. Out-of-cycle urgent payment requests require Controller justification via the regional **Urgent Payment Intake Form** (*Geography*, *Operating Unit*, *Requested Date*, *Business Impact*).
